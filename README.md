# F1 (2025)

> *"In the turns, that's where we make up time."*

An upcoming sports action drama film directed by **Joseph Kosinski** (*Top Gun: Maverick*) and produced in collaboration with Formula 1 and seven-time World Champion **Lewis Hamilton**.

---

## Main Cast & Characters

| Actor | Character | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brad Pitt** | Sonny Hayes | Veteran F1 driver coming out of retirement |
| **Damson Idris** | Joshua Pearce | Young rookie driver and teammate |
| **Kerry Condon** | Kate | APXGP Technical Director |
| **Javier Bardem** | Ruben | APXGP Team Principal |

---

## Key Plot Highlights

1. **The Comeback:** Former 1990s F1 star *Sonny Hayes* suffers a major crash, forcing him into retirement.
2. **The Assignment:** Decades later, his former teammate invites him to join **APXGP**, a fictional 11th team on the grid.
3. **The Mission:** Mentorship of rising prodigy *Joshua Pearce* while proving APXGP can contend against top teams.

---

## Production Trivia

* **Real-World Filming:** Shot during actual **Formula 1 Grand Prix weekends** (such as Silverstone and Abu Dhabi).
* **Modified Cars:** The production team used modified **Formula 2** cars outfitted with real IMAX camera rigs.
* **Authentic Audio:** Audio was recorded live on track to capture exact engine roars.
